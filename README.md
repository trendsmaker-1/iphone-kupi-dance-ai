## What is the iPhone Kupi dance meme?

- **The source.** On October 23, 2026, TikTok creator Win William (@win.william_official) posted a 15-second clip captioned "iPhone 5555": in a hotel room at night he holds a folded phone up to the camera, opens it into a big screen, stands up, dances, tosses the screen and catches it.
- **The song.** The sound is "Базовый минимум" ("Basic Minimum") by SABI and MIA BOYKA, listed on streaming services as "iphone kupi (bare minimum)". "Айфон купи" (iPhone kupi) means "buy me an iPhone", which is why the clip pairs it with a flashy phone.
- **The AI versions.** Within the hour, people started swapping someone else into the clip with AI. One of the first passed 14 million views.

More on the meme and the lyrics: [the iPhone Kupi meme, explained](https://iphonekupidanceai.com/what-is-the-iphone-kupi-dance-meme).

- A clear, well-lit photo with the face toward the camera, no sunglasses or heavy filters.
- One person per photo. A photo that shows the outfit gives the dance more to work with.
- Only use photos of yourself or of adults who agreed. No celebrities, public figures or minors.
